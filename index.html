ABOUT ALALM ELAKHADAR

Professional outdoor solutions designed for UAE environments.

ALALM ELAKHADAR LANDSCAPING L.L.C is a landscaping and external works company based in Abu Dhabi, UAE. We provide coordinated solutions for landscape and garden design, execution, irrigation, planting, paving, gravel and related civil works.

Our work combines practical site execution with attention to landscape appearance, irrigation performance, planting quality, finishing and long-term usability.

OUR SERVICES

Complete landscaping & external works

Professional services for gardens, villas, commercial properties, streetscapes and development projects.

01

Civil Works

External civil and landscape-support works including excavation, site preparation, levels, concrete support, edging, foundations, kerbs, drainage-related preparation and finishing works.

02

Paving / Interlock Installation

Professional preparation and installation of interlock paving, walkways, pedestrian areas, borders, kerbstones and hardscape finishes with accurate alignment and durable finishing.

03

Irrigation Works — Manual & Automatic

Manual and automatic irrigation systems including mainlines, laterals, valves, fittings, outlets, connections, underground pipe networks, distribution and practical watering layouts.

04

Landscape Designing & Execution

Landscape concepts and complete site execution combining softscape, hardscape, decorative gravel, planting, pathways and irrigation into functional outdoor environments.

05

Garden Designing & Execution

Garden development from ground preparation through planting, borders, turf, decorative stone, irrigation and finishing, tailored to the property and intended use.

06

Plants, Shrubs & Grass

Supply and installation of plants, shrubs, trees and grass/turf with soil preparation, correct placement, establishment and landscape care.

07

Gravel & Decorative Stone

Decorative gravel and landscape stone installation, patterns, edging and clean finishing for roadsides, gardens, medians and outdoor spaces.

08

Landscape Maintenance

Garden and landscape upkeep including planting care, turf care, irrigation checks, pruning, cleaning and ongoing presentation support.
